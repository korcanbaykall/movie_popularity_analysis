# AI Usage Disclosure

This document describes my limited use of AI tools during the project, as required by the DSA 210 course guidelines.

The analysis, code, and conclusions in this project are my own work. I used Claude (chat) only as a supporting tool in three narrow ways:

- **Background research.** Before starting the analysis, I occasionally asked clarifying questions about statistical concepts (for example, when a non-parametric test is more appropriate than a parametric one, or how Spearman correlation differs from Pearson). These conversations helped me choose the right methods, but the decisions and the final implementation were mine.
- **Debugging.** When I ran into a Python or pandas error I could not immediately resolve, I sometimes pasted the traceback into Claude to help me understand what was going wrong. The fixes were then applied and verified by me.
- **Proofreading.** I used Claude to catch typos and small grammar issues in the README and notebook markdown cells. No section of the writing was generated by AI; it was only edited for clarity after I had written it.
